7. INTERPRETATION NOTES
==============================================================================

1. Static identification uses steady-state windows from the 14-level PWM sweeps.
2. cmd_v is NOT treated as measured robot velocity.
3. RPM is treated as the primary propulsion output.
4. Dynamic tau values describe the observed motor/drive response under this experiment.
5. Coast-down results do not by themselves provide unique Coulomb/viscous friction coefficients.
6. Driver current/power are bus-level quantities because the instrumentation is shared.
7. The analyzer does not claim parameter identifiability where the recorded data do not support it.
